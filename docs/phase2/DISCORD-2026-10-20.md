## Implemented and tested foundations

- Routing accepts structured bot mentions and replies bound to this bot’s durable outbox in the exact channel. Raw mention text, forged embedded reply authors, foreign guilds, webhooks and self-messages do not establish admission.
- Explicit admin IDs govern DM admission. Other DM users receive a redirect disposition without carrying their message content into admitted work. Actual redirect delivery remains pending.
- The connector journal refuses an Echo conversation archive and binds itself to one bot identity. Inbox originals and delivery receipts are immutable. Duplicate intake and conflicting identities are distinguished.
- Outbox send intent is committed before network effects. Restart never blindly resends an in-progress or uncertain delivery. Sent-message records supply reply-target provenance.
- Gateway dispatch intake and sequence advancement commit atomically. Connection generations fence stale sockets after reconnect, duplicate sequences are detected, conflicting receipts roll back, and ignored dispatches retain no message text.

Native tests cover these boundaries, restart/reopen, stale attempts and rollback. Initial compile failures are retained privately alongside subsequent passing builds. The live resume test qualifies a deliberate short disconnect, not every close code, offline interval, frame-fragmentation or delivery scenario. Continuous production scheduling and delivery remain pending.
