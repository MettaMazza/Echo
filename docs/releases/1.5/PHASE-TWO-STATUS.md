## Owner-dependent closure rule

A task that truly requires the owner's physical authentication, account consent or joint decision is marked **closed on Codex side — awaiting owner**, with the unperformed action named. It does not become an implementation failure, a fabricated passing test or permission to skip independently completable work. Microphone acceptance and final joint prompts are explicit examples. Root-controller activation must retain the same distinction.

## Continuation order

Reconcile the remaining Step 10 criteria, then finish Step 11 against actual implemented backends and proceed through the original dependent steps. Existing later foundations may be reused. Bugs in existing Phase One/Two behavior may be fixed when encountered, then return to the plan. Newly discovered work outside the agreed scope belongs in the Phase Three sketch, not an enlarged Phase Two checklist.
